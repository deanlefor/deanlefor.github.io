Read. Argue. Reconsider.

Special Issue: Classics Reconsidered

Given what we know today, does each argument still hold up as an important and defensible contribution?

Make your case

One voice.
Three minutes.
Four ingredients.

Choose one spokesperson. Prepare one statement containing these four elements.

Preparation About six minutes to work.

  1. Claim

    Give your group’s answer to its assigned question.

  2. Reading

    Use a specific passage to support your reasoning.

  3. Current example

    Show how the argument applies, or falls short, today, and what we have learned since.

  4. Objection

    Anticipate an objection to your position and respond to it.

The running order

  1. WaldoSupport → Critique → Vote
  2. SimonSupport → Critique → Vote

03 Friedrich and Finer restoration case

A fictional case

Classroom Case · November 9, 2026

Friedrich & Finer: Responsibility Under Pressure

The situation

An island without power.
One corridor first.

A hurricane has left an island without power. Crews can restore only one major corridor first.

APlan A

Protect vulnerable residents.

Restore rural hospitals, water facilities, and nearby communities first to protect vulnerable residents.

Defended by Group 1 & Group 2

BPlan B

Restore the relief corridor.

Restore the metropolitan port and supply corridor first to reach more residents and help move relief supplies across the island. Rural facilities would rely on backup generators where available.

Defended by Group 3 & Group 4

The decision is still open

Both plans are lawful and technically feasible. The governor has authority to set priorities but has not decided. You are career administrators advising the governor, who has asked for your advice.
