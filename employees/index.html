EXPLORE ALTERNATIVES

Allocation assistant

Divide flexible funding approximately in proportion to each role's priority weight. A balanced recommendation, not a mathematical optimum.

What does “optimal” mean here?

Every person must be a whole number. Headcount and funding locks and each role's minimum/maximum are hard constraints. Priority weights range from 1 to 10, with larger numbers meaning greater preference.

Weighted positions maximizes the sum of priority × number of staff, which may concentrate hiring in one cheaper or higher-weighted role. Total positions ignores priorities in its main objective. Minimum unspent tries to use as much of the available budget as possible, breaking ties by priority. Balanced spreads available funding by weights and is a heuristic.

For manageable whole-dollar cost combinations the last three objectives use an exact integer optimization. If the search space is too large, the tool clearly labels its result as approximate.

Use full employment cost.

Use consistent estimates covering salary and benefits, and any position-specific overhead you intend to include. The model does not estimate these for you.

Locks do different things.

Headcount locks keep an exact number of employees. Funding locks reserve dollars, then fund the largest whole number of positions they can afford.

Tradeoffs need context.

Optimization is arithmetic, not an assessment of mission effectiveness, legal staffing requirements, skills, workload, or service levels.
