How are recommendations calculated?

Locks and staffing minimums and maximums are hard constraints. Larger priority weights indicate a stronger preference. Maximum weighted positions, maximum total positions, and minimum unspent budget are solved exactly for manageable search spaces, otherwise approximately. The balanced option is always a heuristic. This is budgeting arithmetic, not an assessment of workforce performance or mission requirements.
